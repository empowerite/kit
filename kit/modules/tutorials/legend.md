## Using it

- `?` or `F1`  show or hide it
- It names **only** the glyphs on the screen now, from the top left down.
- "click to …" marks a glyph you can click.
- `↖` `↗` `↙` `↘`  click one to move it to that corner.
- It takes no other key, so the rows under it keep working.

## One glyph, two meanings

A glyph means what its line says:

- `·`  a separator on the menu bar. A sortable column on the rule.
- `▼`  the presets menu on the control line. A sort, down, on the rule.

The legend is on the apps: Flow, Issues, Changes, and the rest.
