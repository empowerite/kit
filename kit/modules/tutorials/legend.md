## Using it

- `?` or `F1`  show or hide it
- It names **only** the glyphs on the screen now, from the top left down.
- "click to …" marks a glyph you can click.
- `↖` `↗` `↙` `↘`  click one to move it to that corner.
- It takes no other key, so the rows under it keep working.

## A sort, and a menu

A sort is always a small number after a column's name on the rule: raised, `¹`, for up; lowered, `₁`, for down.
The number is its place among the sorts. A sort is never a full-size triangle.

- `·`  a column on the rule that can sort, and is not sorted. A dot between items is only a separator, and is not
  listed.
- `▼`  the presets menu on the control line. It opens a menu; it is not a sort.
