```text
                                              🔎 /search
```

It stands at the right of the menu bar, on every screen.

## Keys

- `/`  focus the search. A click does the same.
- Type: the [rows](kittui:tutorials/rows), or cards, narrow as you go. On a page, as the introduction or a tutorial,
  every line stays and each match is highlighted.
- `n` `N`  on a page, the next match and the one before
- `⏎` `↓` `esc` `⇥`  back to the rows. The filter stays.
- `⌃U`  clear it
- `⌃R`  recall a past search

## What it matches

- On an [app](kittui:welcome/terms/application): any column of the rows
- On the [help](kittui:help): a command, its group, its description
- On the [tutorials](kittui:tutorials): a title, an audience, a summary, an id

To see everything again, clear the search with `⌃U`.
