```text
                                              🔎 /search
```

It stands at the right of the menu bar, on every screen.

## Keys

- `/`  focus the search. A click does the same.
- Type: the rows, or cards, narrow as you go.
- `⏎` `↓` `esc` `⇥`  back to the rows. The filter stays.
- `⌃U`  clear it
- `⌃R`  recall a past search

## What it matches

- On an app: any column of the rows
- On the help: a command, its group, its description
- On the tutorials: a title, an audience, a summary, an id

To see everything again, clear the search with `⌃U`.
